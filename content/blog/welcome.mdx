The question "Who am I?" is one that has a very philosophical tone.

I could, of course, start with the obvious things about me. I am 16 and a sophomore. I spend a lot of my time at school, working on projects, doing things with Civil Air Patrol, and trying to learn things I don't know yet.

But that still begs the question, "Who am I?"

I like making things rather than just talking about them. My projects often start as random ideas and sometimes become something much bigger than I anticipate.

I think I am fairly competitive. If I want to do something, I want to try to be the best. Sometimes this is useful; other times, it just adds to the work.

In my future, I want to pursue aerospace. I don't know what sector, but I know it will be aerospace.

For now, I just want to learn, build, and see where it takes me.

This blog will serve as a notebook to document that.
